Changing the form of taxation – deadlines, rules and most common mistakes

The form of taxation requires periodic verification. Check the deadlines, rules and data that are worth comparing before changing.

Taxes January 11, 2022 Liber Pro Accounting

The form of taxation should not be left unverified in subsequent years as the company's revenues, costs and operating model change. From the perspective of the accounting office, it is crucial to compare business data, health insurance contributions and the method of document recording before making a decision to change to the next tax year.

When can you change the form of taxation?

Deadline: by the 20th day of the month after receiving the first income

You submit the notification of a change in the form of taxation to the tax office by the 20th day of the month following the month in which you obtained your first income in a given tax year. After this date, the selected form is valid for the whole year - without the possibility of change.

Example: if you receive your first income in 2027 in January, you have until February 20, 2027 to report a change in the form of taxation. If you start earning money only in March, you have until April 20.

What data is worth comparing before changing?

Changing the form of taxation only makes sense if your situation has changed. Just "I want to pay less" without analysis is a recipe for error. The most common reasons why a change is justified:

Cost increase → KPiR

  • You leased a car or equipment
  • You rented an office or warehouse
  • You hired an employee
  • Spending on marketing or training has increased

Decrease in costs → Lump sum

  • You've paid off the lease and finished investing
  • You have moved to a purely service model
  • You gave up the office and work remotely
  • Costs account for less than 20-25% of revenues

Changing the business model

  • You switched from services to trade (different flat rate)
  • You have expanded your activities with new PKD
  • You changed your customer profile from B2C to B2B

Revenue growth

  • Revenues exceeded the threshold for lump-sum health insurance contributions
  • The income exceeded PLN 120,000 - the 32% scale is no longer attractive
  • You are planning dynamic growth and want to optimize your tax in advance

Same entrepreneur, same industry. Year 1 - Lump sum is definitely better. Year 2 - after the increase in costs, KPiR starts winning. If it did not change the form, it would overpay over PLN 1,400 a year only in tax - without taking into account the difference in health insurance premiums, which is often even more significant.

Health insurance premium – an important element of comparison

In recent years, it is the health insurance premium that often determines the profitability of the form of taxation - more than the tax itself. The difference is fundamental:

  • Lump sum — health insurance premium depends on revenue thresholds. With revenues up to PLN 60,000 per year, you pay approximately PLN 376/month, with PLN 60,000-300,000 per month. approx. PLN 627/month, over PLN 300,000 approx. PLN 1,128/month (indicative values ​​2026). The premium increases dramatically when the threshold is exceeded.
  • Linear KPiR — the contribution is 4.9% of income. If your income is low, you pay little, and if your income is high, you pay a lot, but proportionally.
  • KPiR scale — the contribution is 9% of income. The highest, but tax scale gives lower tax for income up to PLN 120,000 and access to reliefs.

An analysis without a health insurance premium is always incomplete. There are situations where the lump sum tax is lower, but the total burden (tax + contribution) is higher than for KPiR. And vice versa.

When may a change in form not bring the expected effect?

  • When the costs are stable for years and analysis shows that the current form is optimal
  • When there are differences between forms minimalne (less than several hundred PLNs per year) and it is not worth incurring the costs of reorganization
  • When change complicates the business without real financial benefits - e.g. switching to KPiR at very low costs just to "have full records"
  • When the company faces other challenges that are more important than tax optimization

The most common mistakes when changing the form of taxation

Mistake 1. "I stay as I was"

Lack of annual analysis means overpaying tax. The company is changing. The form of taxation of a good at the start may be completely suboptimal after three years of growth.

Mistake 2. Calculating only tax, without health insurance contributions

Many online studies only show tax rate comparisons. This is a mistake. The total burden is tax plus health insurance premium. Without both numbers, the result is incomplete and may lead to a wrong decision.

Mistake 3. Decision based on friends' opinions

"My friend switched to a lump sum and pays less" is not an analysis. Each company has a different cost structure, different PKD, different revenues. What works for your friend may be a loss for you.

Error 4. Simulation for one month instead of the whole year

The form of taxation is valid all year round. The decision should be based on a projection of revenues and costs for 12 months - taking into account seasonality, planned investments and changes in the business.

FAQ – most frequently asked questions

Can I change the lump sum to KPiR?

Yes, from the new tax year. The change must be reported by the 20th day of the month after receiving the first income in a given year.

Is it possible to change the KPiR to a lump sum?

Yes, if the conditions are met - the type of activity must be covered by a lump sum and revenues cannot exceed the limit of EUR 2 million. Not every business can benefit from the lump sum.

Does the change affect ZUS?

Yes, especially for health insurance premiums. The lump sum depends on income thresholds, and the KPiR depends on income. Changing the form may mean a significantly higher or lower health insurance premium.

Can you change your form during the year?

No. The change is only possible at the beginning of the tax year, before the filing deadline. During the year, the selected form applies without exception.

Is it worth analyzing your form every year?

Definitely yes. The company is changing - costs are rising, revenues and the business model are changing. The form chosen a few years ago may not correspond to the current structure of costs and revenues.

Summary

The form of taxation is not a one-time decision. This is an element of company financial management that should be regularly verified. A well-chosen one reduces tax, improves financial liquidity and provides predictability. Wrongly selected - it generates losses and limits development opportunities.

If you are not sure whether the current form is optimal for you - it is best to do a short simulation based on real data of your company. It takes just a few minutes, and the savings can be significant throughout the year.
