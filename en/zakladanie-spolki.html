FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to set up a limited liability company?

For S24 registration, the court fee is PLN 250 and publication in the Court and Commercial Gazette costs PLN 100. For registration through PRS, the court fee is PLN 500 plus PLN 100 for publication. Notarial, power-of-attorney and professional service costs may also apply. The minimum share capital is PLN 5,000. Check the current official fees.

Limited liability company and sole proprietorship – basic differences

A sole proprietorship and a limited liability company differ in liability, taxation, operating costs and accounting obligations. A limited liability company requires full accounting and registration in KRS. The right choice depends on business risk, the shareholders’ plans and financial forecasts, so we compare the options individually.

How long does it take to register a company?

Registration time depends on the selected route, document completeness and the workload of the relevant registry court. S24 simplifies standard documentation, but it does not guarantee registration by a specific date. After registration, additional filings such as NIP-8 and beneficial-owner reporting may be required.

What is the difference between S24 registration and notarial registration?

S24 uses an official agreement template and electronic signatures, which makes it suitable for standard provisions. A notarial agreement offers more flexibility and is required, for example, when a contribution in kind is involved. We help select the route that matches the shareholders’ actual needs.

Must a limited liability company keep full accounting?

Yes. A limited liability company must keep full accounting records regardless of revenue and prepare annual financial statements. This involves more formal obligations than a sole proprietorship, while also providing more detailed management information.

Does a sole-shareholder limited liability company pay ZUS?

As a rule, the sole shareholder of a limited liability company is covered by social insurance as a person conducting non-agricultural activity. Multi-shareholder structures require an individual assessment that considers the actual distribution of shares and current ZUS and court practice.

Can you take over the operation of an existing company?

Yes, we take over accounting services for companies at any time, regardless of the stage of activity. We take over documentation from the previous office or internal accounting department. During the first meeting, we discuss the company's situation and prepare an offer.
