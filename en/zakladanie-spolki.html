    Frequently asked questions pytania

    How much does it cost to set up a limited liability company?

    Registration of a limited liability company via the S24 system (online) costs PLN 250 court fee. Notarial registration costs approximately PLN 500-1,500 court fee plus notary fee (depending on the amount of share capital). Minimum share capital of a limited liability company is PLN 5,000. In addition, there are the costs of legal and accounting services - contact us for a free quote.

    Limited liability company and sole proprietorship – basic differences

    JDG is simpler, cheaper and less formal - perfect for start-ups and small companies. Limited liability company limits the personal liability of partners to the capital contributed, which is important in the case of greater business risk or when attracting investors. Limited liability company it is also better when the company generates high profits - it is possible to optimize taxes through a combination of CIT and dividends. We present individually.

    How long does it take to register a company?

    Registration via S24 (KRS online system) usually takes 1-7 business days from submitting the application. Notarial registration takes longer - usually 7-14 days. After entry in the National Court Register, a NIP and REGON number are automatically assigned. The application to ZUS must be submitted within 7 days of registration.

    What is the difference between S24 registration and notarial registration?

    S24 is a faster and cheaper online mode - it can be used when the partnership agreement is standard (without special provisions). Notarial registration is necessary when the partners want to make a contribution in kind to the company (e.g. real estate, enterprise) or when the contract contains non-standard provisions requiring a notarial form. We implement choose the right mode.

    Is the limited liability company must keep full accounting?

    Yes, limited liability company is obliged to keep full accounting (Accounting Books) regardless of the amount of revenue. This means, among others: obligation to prepare an annual financial report submitted to the National Court Register and the tax office. This is a higher accounting cost than with JDG, but it also provides much more management information about the company's condition.

    Is a sole proprietorship limited liability company must pay ZUS?

    Yes, the sole shareholder of a limited liability company. is treated as a person conducting non-agricultural activities and is subject to mandatory ZUS (social and health) contributions. In the case of a company with at least two partners, the situation is more favorable - the partners do not pay social contributions for holding shares. This is an important argument when choosing the ownership structure of the company.

    Can you take over the operation of an existing company?

    Yes, we take over accounting services for companies at any time, regardless of the stage of activity. We take over documentation from the previous office or internal accounting department. The first meeting is free of charge - during it we discuss the company's situation and prepare an offer.
