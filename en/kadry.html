FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does HR and payroll services cost?

The price depends primarily on the number of employees and the complexity of settlements. For a company employing 1-3 people, the monthly cost of HR and payroll services is usually lower than the cost of one hour of a lawyer's work. Contact us – we will prepare a free quote for your company.

Can I outsource only HR without accounting?

Yes, we can provide HR and payroll services independently of accounting, even if the latter is run by another office. Many of our clients use only HR services. Of course, we can also take over the entire service - then the costs are usually lower.

What does keeping personal files involve?

Personnel files have five parts: A, B, C, D and E. They cover recruitment, employment, termination, disciplinary records and, where applicable, sobriety or similar-substance testing records. We maintain personnel documentation in accordance with the applicable rules, in paper or electronic form.

How does remote payroll settlement work?

Each month, you send us information about working hours, leave and other variable pay components. We use it to calculate salaries, prepare payroll and arrange payment data for employees and ZUS. The process is handled remotely through a secure client portal, without paper documents or office visits.

Do you handle documentation when hiring the first employee?

Yes. We help organise employment documentation, prepare the relevant agreements and ZUS notifications, and set up working-time records. We determine the required documents and notifications for the specific form of employment.

Do you deal with PPK (Employee Capital Plans)?

Yes. We support PPK administration, including contribution calculations, transfers and employee declarations. The duty to establish PPK depends on statutory conditions and exemptions. These can include eligible micro-enterprises whose employees all opt out, and employers meeting the conditions for a PPE exemption. We check the requirements for each employer.

How quickly can you take over the HR management of my company?

Typically, it takes 3-7 business days to take over the service. As a matter of urgency, we can start even faster. We take over the documentation from the previous office or HR employee - without interruption in serving your employees.
