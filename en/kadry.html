FAQ

Frequently asked questions pytania

How much does HR and payroll services cost?

The price depends primarily on the number of employees and the complexity of settlements. For a company employing 1-3 people, the monthly cost of HR and payroll services is usually lower than the cost of one hour of a lawyer's work. Contact us – we will prepare a free quote for your company.

Can I outsource only HR without accounting?

Yes, we can provide HR and payroll services independently of accounting, even if the latter is run by another office. Many of our clients use only HR services. Of course, we can also take over the entire service - then the costs are usually lower.

What does keeping personal files involve?

The personal file consists of four parts (A, B, C, D) and contains documents related to applying for employment, work history, termination of the employment relationship and organizational responsibility. We conduct them in accordance with the regulation of the Minister of Family - both in electronic and paper versions.

How does remote payroll settlement work?

Every month you send us information about hours worked, holidays and other variable remuneration components. On this basis, we calculate remuneration, prepare payrolls and prepare transfers for employees and ZUS. The entire process takes place via a secure digital desktop - without papers or visits.

Do you handle documentation when hiring the first employee?

Yes, we will guide you through the entire process: selecting the appropriate form of employment (employment contract, mandate contract, contract for specific work, B2B), preparing the contract, reporting to ZUS as a contribution payer, registering the employee with ZUS and PIP, and configuring working time records. The first job can be complicated - with us it will go smoothly.

Do you deal with PPK (Employee Capital Plans)?

Yes, we provide comprehensive PPK services: program implementation, calculating employer and employee contributions, transferring funds to a financial institution and handling employee resignations. PPK is mandatory for all employers except those running PPE.

How quickly can you take over the HR management of my company?

Typically, it takes 3-7 business days to take over the service. As a matter of urgency, we can start even faster. We take over the documentation from the previous office or HR employee - without interruption in serving your employees.
