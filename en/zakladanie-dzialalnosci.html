    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions pytania

    How much does it cost to start a business?

    Registration of JDG through CEIDG is completely free. There are no fees for entry in the register, NIP or REGON. The only costs are a possible license or permit fee (if the activity requires it) and ZUS contributions, which start one month after registration. We take care of the formalities related to registration and preparation of documents.

    How long does it take to register a company?

    The entry in CEIDG is effective on the day of submitting the application - if you submit it before 5 p.m., the company legally exists on the same day. Registration with ZUS should take place within 7 days from the date of commencement of business activity. Registration as a VAT payer usually takes 1-3 business days.

    Which form of taxation should you choose - general rules, linear or lump sum?

    It depends on expected revenues, costs and industry. A lump sum is beneficial when you have low costs and high income (e.g. IT services, lease). A flat tax of 19% is profitable for high incomes above approximately PLN 120,000 per year. General rules (tax scale) are best when you plan to use reliefs or your income is low. We present the optimal choice based on your specific situation - free of charge during the first consultation.

    Do I have to register as a VAT payer immediately?

    No. most new companies can benefit from VAT exemption until their annual turnover exceeds PLN 200,000. However, there are situations when VAT registration is immediately beneficial or obligatory (e.g. in cooperation with other VAT companies, in certain industries). We present the formal scope of VAT registration and required notifications.

    What are PKD codes and how to choose them?

    PKD (Polish Classification of Activities) are codes defining the type of business activity. One of them is the main (dominant), the rest are additional ones. The choice of PKD is important when it comes to lump sum rates, the possibility of using tax reliefs and settlements with ZUS. We indicate the scope of data required to register your business.

    Does a new company have to pay ZUS from the first month?

    New entrepreneurs can take advantage of reliefs: for the first 6 months it is possible to take advantage of the so-called start-up relief (no social contributions, only health insurance contribution), and for the next 24 months from preferential ZUS contributions (approx. 30% of the standard rate). In total, for 2.5 years you pay much less than standard premiums. We process reports to the appropriate institutions.

    Can I start a company if I am employed full-time?

    Yes, running a JDG parallel to working full-time is completely legal. If your full-time salary is at least equal to the minimum wage, you do not have to pay social security contributions from your activity (only health insurance). This is a significant saving for people who combine full-time employment with their own company. We process the application to ZUS in accordance with the data provided during registration.
