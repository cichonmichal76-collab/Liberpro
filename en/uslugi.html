FAQ

Frequently asked questions pytania

What services does Liber Pro Accounting offer?

We offer a full range of services: full accounting (KH, KPiR, lump sum, VAT, CIT, PIT, JPK), HR and payroll services (payrolls, ZUS, PFRON, personal files), establishing JDG and companies (CEIDG, KRS), tax and accounting services and representation before offices. Everything in one place – one caregiver, full service.

How much does the company's accounting service cost?

The price depends on the legal form, the number of documents per month, the scope of services and the complexity of settlements. For small lump-sum JDGs, prices are affordable - for limited liability companies. with full accounting higher education. We always prepare an individual quote after a free consultation. We do not apply hidden fees - everything is clearly specified in the contract.

Can I outsource only some of the services - e.g. only VAT or only HR?

Yes, we can take over the selected scope of service independently of the others. Many clients use only HR services, only VAT settlements or only assistance in setting up a company. We adapt the scope of cooperation to your needs.

How quickly do you respond to questions and messages?

We respond within one business day - in most cases much faster. Each client is assigned a representative who knows his company and answers questions on an ongoing basis. We do not transfer clients between employees - you have one point of contact.

Do you offer KSeF (National e-Invoice System) support?

Yes, we are prepared to support KSeF. Each Liber Pro client receives access to a digital dashboard that supports issuing and receiving structured invoices. We also support the configuration of integration with the client's invoicing systems. KSeF will become mandatory for all companies - it is worth being ready earlier.

How to start working with Liber Pro?

Call or write - the first initial conversation is free and non-binding. Tel: +48 517-765-128. After the conversation, we will prepare an offer tailored to your company. If you decide to cooperate, we will take over all documentation and configure your desktop within a few days.

Accounting services

Comprehensive service for companies
Kraków, Liszki, Skawina and all of Poland

Liber Pro is an accounting office based in Piekary near Krakow. We offer a full range of accounting, HR, payroll and consulting services for companies - regardless of their size, industry and location. We serve entrepreneurs from Krakow, Skawina, Liszki, Czernichów and all over Poland remotely.

Our services include full accounting (KH) and simplified accounting (KPiR, lump sum), HR and payroll services, setting up sole proprietorships and companies, as well as handling formalities related to registration and ongoing accounting and tax services. Each client has a dedicated account manager and access to a digital document management desktop with KSeF support.

The owner of the office has Accounting Certificate of the Minister of Finance. the highest confirmation of professional qualifications in Poland, awarded by the Minister of Finance after passing the state examination. We have liability insurance for the accounting office. The first consultation is always free.
