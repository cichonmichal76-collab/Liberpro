How much does an accounting office cost?

The price depends on the legal form of the company, the number of documents and the scope of services. For a sole proprietorship on a lump sum basis, prices start from several dozen PLNs per month. For companies with full accounting, the rates are higher. We offer a free quote - contact us and we will prepare an offer tailored to your company.

Can I change my accounting office during the tax year?

Yes, you can do this at any time of the year. We handle the entire process of taking over the documentation from the previous office. We will ensure a smooth transition without the risk of interruption in service or loss of important tax deadlines.

Do you offer remote service for companies outside Krakow?

Yes, we serve companies from all over Poland fully remotely. You send us documents electronically and we communicate by e-mail and telephone. No office visits are required. We have clients from Warsaw, Wrocław, Gdańsk and many other cities.

What is the difference between full accounting and KPiR?

The Book of Revenues and Expenditures (KPiR) is a simplified form of records for small businesses based on a flat tax or general principles. Full accounting (Accounting Books) is mandatory for limited liability companies, joint-stock companies and companies whose revenues exceed EUR 2 million per year. Full accounting also provides much more management information about the company's condition.

What is JPK and who must submit it?

JPK (Unified Audit File) is an electronic format for transmitting accounting data to the tax office. Mandatory for all VAT taxpayers - submitted monthly in the form of a JPK_VAT file. At the request of the tax office, you may also be obliged to send other JPK structures (including accounting books). We prepare and send all JPK files for our clients.

Is the accounting office liable for errors in settlements?

Yes, we have mandatory liability insurance for the accounting office. This means that in the event of an error on our part that causes financial harm to the client, the insurer will cover the resulting costs. This is an important guarantee of security that is not available to people conducting accounting without formal qualifications.

What does the first meeting with an accounting office look like?

The first initial conversation is free and lasts approximately 30-45 minutes - by phone or via Teams. We discuss the legal form of the company, the number of documents, the specificity of the industry and the scope of services. On this basis, we prepare a cooperation offer.
