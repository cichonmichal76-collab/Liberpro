Privacy policy

Information on personal data processing and the use of cookies on liberpro.pl.

1. Personal data administrator

The administrator of personal data is Monika Cichoń running a business under the name Liber Pro Accounting, contact: kontakt(at)liberpro.pl, tel. +48 517-765-128.

2. What data do we collect?

We only collect data when you provide it to us:

  • Contact form. name and surname, e-mail address, telephone number (optional), message content. The data is necessary to respond to your inquiry.
  • Google Analytics. analytical data about website traffic (anonymous): number of visits, time spent on the website, device type, country. This data does not allow the identification of a specific person.

3. Purpose and legal basis for processing

  • Query handling. art. 6 section 1 letter b GDPR (necessity to take action before concluding a contract) or Art. 6 section 1 letter f GDPR (legitimate interest).
  • Analytics (Google Analytics). art. 6 section 1 letter a of the GDPR (consent), expressed by accepting cookies in the banner.

4. Cookies

The website uses the following cookies:

  • Essential. remembering your choice regarding cookies (cookie_consent). They do not require consent.
  • Analytical (Google Analytics). collect anonymous data about the use of the website. Activated only with your consent. You can reject them in the cookie banner or withdraw your consent at any time.

You can manage cookies in your browser settings. Disabling analytical cookies does not affect the operation of the website.

5. Data Sharing

Data from the contact form are not made available to third parties. Analytical data (Google Analytics) are processed by Google LLC in accordance with their privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Data storage period

  • Data from the contact form – until the correspondence is completed, no longer than 3 years.
  • Analytical data (Google Analytics). 14 months (GA default setting).
  • Consent cookies. 365 days.

7. Your rights

You have the following rights:

  • The right to access data
  • The right to rectify data
  • The right to delete data ("right to be forgotten")
  • The right to restrict processing
  • The right to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting the legality of previous processing)
  • The right to lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (uodo.gov.pl)

To exercise your rights, please contact: kontakt(at)liberpro.pl

8. Security

The website uses the HTTPS protocol (SSL/TLS encryption). The contact form is spam protected. The data is not transferred outside the European Economic Area, with the exception of Google Analytics (Google LLC, USA). the transfer is based on standard contractual clauses.

9. Changes to the privacy policy

We reserve the right to change this policy. We will inform you about significant changes via a message on the website. Date last updated: March 2026.
