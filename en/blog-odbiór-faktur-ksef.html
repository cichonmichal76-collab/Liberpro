Receipt of invoices from KSeF - obligation and organization of the process in the company

Check how to organize the receipt of invoices from KSeF, document circulation and forwarding cost invoices to accounting.

KSeF September 6, 2025 Liber Pro Accounting

After the introduction of the mandatory KSeF, it is important not only to issue invoices correctly, but also to receive them on an ongoing basis and include them in the document flow in the company. From the perspective of the accounting office, the most important are: completeness of cost documents, timely submission of them to the records and data consistency between KSeF, the accounting system and VAT settlements.

The most important rule for collecting invoices at KSeF

The invoice exists at the time of acceptance by KSeF - not when the PDF is sent

This is a fundamental change compared to the existing rules. The invoice does not have to be sent to you via an encrypted channel. You may never see it in your mailbox. And yet - it already exists, is considered served and produces legal effects from the moment it is accepted by the KSeF system.

Your contractor issues an invoice in his system, KSeF accepts it - and from that moment the tax deadlines start to run. There is no waiting for your receipt, confirmation or response. The invoice is delivered automatically.

What does failure to receive an invoice from KSeF mean?

1

The document will not be included in the current cost register

The invoice does not go to accounting, so the cost is not settled. The result: an inflated tax base and higher income tax than you should pay.

2

There is a risk of delayed recognition of input VAT

Without an invoice in the system, there is no basis for deducting input VAT. This means that you pay more VAT to the office than you should - and you cannot make a correction later without documentation from KSeF.

3

Discrepancies in VAT records and source documents

The tax office sees all invoices issued to your company in the KSeF system. If your JPK_VAT file does not show these invoices, there is a discrepancy, which is one of the main alarm signals for data verification.

4

Chaos in company documentation

Lack of consistency between invoices in KSeF and documents in the accounting system means problems with monthly closings, annual settlements and possible control.

The most common organizational error after the implementation of KSeF

Error scenario:

✗

The contractor issues an invoice to KSeF

✗

Additionally, it sends PDF via an encrypted channel "for convenience"

✗

The company records PDF from e-mail - as before

✗

The invoice from KSeF is not collected and is not entered into the books

Result: from the point of view of regulations, the company does not have the correct document. Risk of questioning the cost and VAT deduction by the tax office.

How to organize the invoice collection process?

1

The contractor issues an invoice to KSeF

The invoice enters the KSeF system and receives a unique number. From now on it is legally served.

2

The system automatically downloads the invoice from KSeF

An integrated accounting program (e.g. Comarch ERP Optima) periodically checks KSeF and downloads new invoices via API. No manual operation required.

3

The invoice goes to the CTI Customer Dashboard and to the books

The document automatically appears in the Client's Desktop - visible to the company owner and the accounting office. It goes to the VAT purchase register.

4

Cost and VAT are included correctly

Full compliance between KSeF and JPK. No discrepancies. No risk of inspection on this account.

Who is responsible for receiving invoices in the company?

It depends on the organization of the company. In each case, the final responsibility rests with the owner. In practice, invoice collection may be handled by:

  • Company owner — directly through the ksef.gov.pl portal or an integrated program
  • Administration or finance department — after granting permissions by the owner in KSeF
  • Accounting — after granting appropriate permissions. At Liber Pro, we receive invoices on behalf of clients automatically via the CTI Client Dashboard

Regardless of who technically receives the invoices, the responsibility for the correctness of settlements always lies with the company.

Manually downloading invoices from the ksef.gov.pl portal is possible, but carries a high risk of errors and omissions - especially when the company has many contractors. Automatic integration via API is the only solution that eliminates operational risk.

How to protect yourself?

  • Collection automation — integrate your accounting program with KSeF via API. No integration = no automation = risk
  • Internal procedure — determine who is responsible for monitoring received invoices and verifying their correctness
  • Compliance check — regularly compare invoices in KSeF with what is in the accounting system
  • Authorizations for the accounting office — give the office the right to receive invoices in KSeF so that it has access to complete documentation

FAQ – most frequently asked questions

Is an invoice in KSeF valid without my confirmation of receipt?

Yes. The invoice is considered delivered when it is accepted by the KSeF system. Your confirmation is not required - the invoice exists and has legal effects regardless of whether you downloaded it.

Can I post a PDF invoice instead of downloading it from KSeF?

No. PDF is only an auxiliary visualization, not an accounting document. The only legally binding document is the XML file in KSeF. Accounting based solely on PDF may be questioned by the tax office.

Is failure to receive an invoice from KSeF a tax error?

Yes, it may have tax consequences. An invoice not received from KSeF is not entered into accounting - no cost in the tax account and no basis for deducting input VAT.

Can an accounting office collect invoices from KSeF on my behalf?

Yes, if the company owner grants the office appropriate rights in KSeF. At Liber Pro, we receive invoices automatically on behalf of customers through the integrated CTI Customer Dashboard.

Summary

One key rule changes in the KSeF system: you do not have to receive an invoice for it to exist and have legal consequences. Therefore, collecting invoices becomes an obligation - not an option. Companies that do not implement this lose control over costs and risk tax errors with financial consequences.

The solution is simple: automatic integration of the accounting system with KSeF via API and granting permissions to the accounting office. The result: zero manual operations, full documentation compliance and peace of mind during inspections.
