Polish ZUS contribution holiday 2026: submit RWS in August for September

For a September 2026 exemption, the RWS application must be submitted in August. Check the rules, exclusions and ZUS settlement documents.

ZUS8 August 2026Liber Pro Accounting

Planning to use the Polish ZUS contribution holiday for September 2026? The RWS application must be submitted in August 2026. The chosen exemption month always follows the month in which the application is filed. An application submitted in September will not cover September contributions.

The contribution holiday lets an eligible sole trader avoid paying selected social-security contributions for one month in a calendar year. It does not mean a month entirely without ZUS. Health insurance, contributions for employees and contractors, and the required settlement documents still remain.

Key points

  • for September 2026, submit RWS between 1 and 31 August 2026;
  • the application is submitted electronically from the payer account in eZUS;
  • the relief is available for one chosen month per calendar year;
  • it concerns the entrepreneur's own social contributions and, where applicable, voluntary sickness insurance plus the Labour and Solidarity Funds;
  • it does not cover health insurance or contributions for employees and contractors;
  • the business may continue operating, invoicing and earning income;
  • the relief is de minimis aid; and
  • correct ZUS documents are still required for the exemption month.

What is the contribution holiday?

It is an exemption from paying part of an entrepreneur's own ZUS contributions for one selected month each year. The social contributions covered by the exemption are financed by the state budget. The exemption may also cover the Labour Fund and Solidarity Fund where the entrepreneur is required to pay them. The relief month is still taken into account when future pension and disability benefits are determined.

You do not need to suspend the business. You may continue to work, sell goods or services, issue invoices and earn income. The relief is voluntary: the entrepreneur chooses the month.

Which contributions can be covered?

The exemption may include the entrepreneur's pension, disability, accident and voluntary sickness insurance contributions, as well as the Labour Fund and Solidarity Fund where payable. In the relief month, the calculation base is the statutory minimum applicable to that entrepreneur; for Small ZUS Plus, the separate Small ZUS Plus rules apply. You cannot declare a higher base for that month or pay an additional amount above it.

What is not covered?

  • the entrepreneur's health insurance contribution;
  • contributions due for employees;
  • contributions due for contractors;
  • contributions for cooperating persons; and
  • the company's other tax and public-law liabilities.

For that reason, “a month without ZUS” is an oversimplification. The entrepreneur still pays health insurance, and an employer settles staff contributions in the usual way.

Who can use the relief?

An individual running non-agricultural business activity registered in CEIDG, and a court enforcement officer, may use it if all statutory conditions are met.

Insured-person limit

In the month before filing, the entrepreneur may have no more than 10 people registered for the relevant insurance, including themselves. For an application filed in August 2026, ZUS checks July 2026. A person can count towards the limit even if they were insured for only one day of that month.

The limit may include the entrepreneur, employees, contractors (except pupils and students under 26 who are not insured under the contract) and cooperating persons. It also includes people registered for health insurance only. People receiving maternity benefit throughout the whole reference month, or on parental or unpaid leave throughout that month, are excluded.

The entrepreneur's own insurance

The entrepreneur must have been subject to pension, disability and accident insurance from the business for at least one day in the month before filing. For an August 2026 application, this means at least one day in July 2026.

Revenue limit

In one of the two years before the filing year, annual business revenue must not exceed the equivalent of EUR 2 million. For an application filed in 2026, the relevant years are 2024 and 2025. The condition is met if there was no business revenue in both years, or if revenue in at least one of those years did not exceed the limit.

Former employer

The relief is not available where, in the previous calendar year or from 1 January of the filing year until the filing date, the entrepreneur performed for a former employer the same type of work as under employment or cooperative employment in the year the business began or the year before. For an August 2026 application, this means 2025 and the period from 1 January 2026 until filing. This requires an assessment of the actual duties, not simply job titles or contract names.

De minimis aid

The contribution holiday is de minimis aid. The entrepreneur must have enough unused aid available to cover the contributions from which exemption is sought. ZUS states that total de minimis aid received during the previous three years may not exceed EUR 300,000. The relevant information is included in RWS.

Civil partnership partners, preferential contributions and Small ZUS Plus

A civil partnership partner applies as an individual from their own payer account in eZUS, not from the partnership account. This should be checked especially where partners previously settled their own contributions through the partnership account.

The relief may apply both to full social contributions and to preferential contributions or Small ZUS Plus. After approval, settlement uses the appropriate relief code: 05 14 for a base equal to 60% of forecast average pay; 05 74 for preferential contributions based on 30% of minimum pay; and 05 94 for Small ZUS Plus.

Does it cover voluntary sickness insurance?

It can, but the entrepreneur must be covered by voluntary sickness insurance for at least one day both in the filing month and in the month before. For an August 2026 application, check July and August 2026.

How to submit RWS for September 2026

Deadline

Submit the application in August 2026, no later than 31 August. ZUS considers only applications made in the month immediately before the exemption month. An application filed too early or in the planned exemption month will not be considered.

How to file

RWS is filed electronically only, from the payer account in eZUS. It can be submitted by the entrepreneur or a duly authorised representative. The form is partly prefilled with payer data and normally points to the month after filing.

RWS 3.0 in 2026

From 26 June 2026, ZUS has made RWS version 3.0 available. In its de minimis aid section, the entrepreneur selects the PKD classification version: 2007 or 2025. The choice is available until 31 December 2026; after that, PKD 2025 applies.

How much can be saved in 2026?

The amount depends on the contribution basis and voluntary sickness insurance. At the standard minimum base for full ZUS in 2026, social contributions including voluntary sickness insurance and the Labour and Solidarity Funds total approximately PLN 1,926.76. Without voluntary sickness insurance, this is approximately PLN 1,788.29.

For preferential contributions, the approximate total is PLN 456.18 with voluntary sickness insurance or PLN 420.86 without it. For Small ZUS Plus, the amount depends on the individual income-based calculation base. These values use a typical accident-insurance rate of 1.67%. Health insurance remains payable. Documents after approval

Approval does not remove the obligation to settle the month. File, by the applicable deadline, the RCA report with the appropriate relief code and state-financed social contributions; an RCA with the current business code and health insurance contribution; an RCA for other insured people where applicable; and ZUS DRA reflecting the reports. If ZUS does not grant the voluntary sickness-insurance exemption, include that contribution in the relevant RCA.

ZUS may create some documents from the data it holds if none are submitted. This does not ensure a correct settlement for employees, and an automatically calculated health contribution may not reflect the actual situation. Check the documents even after a positive RWS decision.

Common mistakes

  • filing in the wrong month — for September, file in August;
  • assuming that health insurance is cancelled too;
  • omitting employees or health-only insured people from the 10-person limit;
  • not checking available de minimis aid;
  • filing from a civil partnership account rather than an individual's payer account; and
  • not submitting settlement documents for the relief month.

Checklist before sending RWS

  • Have you already used the contribution holiday in 2026?
  • Did the number of insured people stay at or below 10 in July 2026?
  • Were you subject to social insurance from the business for at least one day in July?
  • Do you meet the revenue condition for 2024 or 2025?
  • Are you outside the former-employer exclusion?
  • Is de minimis aid available?
  • Are you using the correct payer account and the correct PKD version in RWS 3.0?
  • Will you prepare the correct RCA and DRA after the decision?

Frequently asked questions

Can I file RWS for September 2026 in September?

No. File in August 2026, the month before the exemption.

Does the relief include health insurance?

No. The health insurance contribution remains payable.

Must I suspend the business?

No. The business can continue operating normally.

Can I apply while employing staff?

Yes, if the insured-person limit is met. The exemption does not cover staff contributions.

Can a civil partnership partner submit RWS?

Yes, from their own payer account in eZUS rather than from the partnership account.

Does the relief apply to Small ZUS Plus?

It may, if the remaining conditions are met. Settlement uses the applicable relief code, including 05 94 for Small ZUS Plus.

Can an accountant file the application?

Yes, with proper authorisation in eZUS.

Are RCA and DRA required?

Yes. The relief month still needs correct settlement documents.

Is the exempt amount taxable income?

No. It is exempt from income tax, but it cannot be deducted again as a paid contribution.
