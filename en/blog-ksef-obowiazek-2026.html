KSeF 2026 – when is the obligation and how to prepare the company

Check when KSeF is mandatory, who it applies to and how to prepare your company to issue and receive e-invoices.

KSeF Updated: July 19, 2026 Liber Pro Accounting

The National e-Invoice System changes the way of issuing, receiving and archiving structured invoices. For companies, the dates of entry into force of the obligation, granting authorizations, preparation of document circulation and adapting the accounting program and internal processes to work with KSeF are of key importance.

What is KSeF?

KSeF is a central IT system operated by the Polish Ministry of Finance. Businesses issue and receive invoices in a standardised XML format using the current FA(3) structure. Each invoice accepted by the system receives a unique KSeF number and is stored for 10 years.

A structured invoice issued by KSeF replaces a traditional paper or PDF invoice. The buyer receives it directly from the system - without having to send it via an encrypted channel or by post.

Since when is KSeF mandatory?

The obligation is being introduced in stages under the current rules:

  • From February 1, 2026 – KSeF issuance is mandatory for taxpayers whose gross sales in 2024 exceeded PLN 200 million.
  • From April 1, 2026 – the obligation covers other businesses, with a temporary exception for the smallest taxpayers.
  • From January 1, 2027 – the exception ends for taxpayers whose monthly gross sales documented by invoices do not exceed PLN 10,000.
  • Receiving invoices through KSeF has been mandatory since February 1, 2026, subject to statutory exceptions. Consumer B2C invoices are not subject to mandatory KSeF issuance.

Current rules and exceptions are published by the Polish Ministry of Finance.

What changes for the company after the entry of KSeF?

  • Issuing invoices. instead of issuing an invoice in the program and sending a PDF, you send the data to KSeF which assigns a number and stores the document
  • Receiving invoices. invoices from contractors go directly to your KSeF account, not through an encrypted channel
  • Storage. you don't have to archive invoices yourself. KSeF stores them for 10 years
  • VAT settlements – in specific cases the regulations provide preferences connected with KSeF; the conditions should be checked for the particular settlement.
  • Invoice details. A structured invoice has a strictly defined structure - required fields cannot be omitted

How to prepare your company for KSeF?

Step 1. Check your invoicing program

Current versions of many popular accounting and invoicing systems support KSeF 2.0. Confirm with the provider that your version is updated, configured correctly and whether an additional module is required.

Step 2. Access KSeF

You access KSeF using an appropriate authentication method, such as a trusted profile, qualified signature or seal, or a KSeF certificate. You may grant an accounting office permissions within the scope agreed for the engagement.

Step 3. Prepare your company details

A structured invoice requires complete data: Tax Identification Number, full company name, address, contractor details. It is worth reviewing the contractor database and filling in the missing Tax Identification Numbers. Without this, issuing an invoice to KSeF will be impossible.

Step 4. Train employees

If more than one person in the company issues invoices, everyone must know the new process. KSeF requires activities other than traditional invoicing: approval in the system, verification of invoice status, handling rejections.

KSeF and cooperation with an accounting office

We organise document handling with CTI accounting software. Clients receive access to the CTI Client Dashboard and, depending on the agreed scope and permissions, can transfer documents and work with KSeF invoices through a web browser.

Frequently asked questions about the mandatory KSeF

Will paper invoices disappear completely?

Not entirely. Paper invoices remain possible for B2C transactions and in situations allowed by the regulations. For domestic B2B transactions, a structured invoice is the general rule, subject to statutory exclusions and transition periods.

What if the KSeF system is unavailable?

The Ministry of Finance provides for an emergency mode - when KSeF is unavailable, offline invoices can be issued according to a special scheme and sent to KSeF after the availability is restored.

What are the penalties for not using KSeF?

Administrative KSeF penalties are not applied during the 2026 transition period. Penalties are provided for from January 1, 2027; their application and amount depend on the current regulations and the circumstances of the violation. Check the current Polish Ministry of Finance guidance.
