KSeF 2026 – checklist for implementation and circulation of invoices

Check how to prepare your company for KSeF: authorizations, receipt of invoices, document circulation, integration and work organization.

KSeF June 15, 2024 Liber Pro Accounting

Wdrożenie KSeF wymaga nie tylko dostępu do systemu, ale również uporządkowania procesu wystawiania i odbioru faktur, nadania uprawnień oraz ustalenia, jak dokumenty będą trafiały do księgowości. From the perspective of an accounting office, the most important are: consistent document circulation, completeness of data and a clear division of responsibilities.

When does KSeF become mandatory?

Large companies

February 1, 2026

VAT taxpayers with revenues above PLN 200 million

Other companies

April 1, 2026

All other VAT taxpayers

In practice, KSeF applies to all VAT taxpayers conducting B2B activities. Invoices to consumers (B2C) remain outside the responsibility of KSeF.

KSeF – company preparation checklist

1

Invoice = XML file in KSeF

The only legally binding invoice is the XML file adopted by KSeF. The PDF is only an auxiliary visualization - it does not have the legal value of an invoice. If you only send a PDF, then from a legal point of view, the invoice does not exist.

2

The moment of issuing the invoice

The invoice only exists after acceptance by KSeF. Sending data to the system and receiving the KSeF number is the moment of issuing it - not clicking "issue" in the program.

3

Receipt of cost invoices

Invoices from contractors will no longer arrive via an encrypted channel or by post. You must download them from KSeF - through the integrated program or directly from the ksef.gov.pl portal. No collection = no costs in accounting.

Powers and roles in KSeF

1 Accounting and invoicing system

  • Does your program support KSeF (API or integration)? Check with your supplier.
  • Does it require an update to the new version?
  • Does it support both issuing and receiving invoices from KSeF?
  • Comarch ERP Optima - full KSeF integration available from version 2024.x

2 Permissions and access

  • Who in the company will issue invoices? Must have permissions granted in KSeF.
  • Who will collect cost invoices from KSeF?
  • Does the accounting office have the authority to act on your behalf?
  • Authorizations are granted by the company owner via ksef.gov.pl (trusted profile or electronic signature).

3 Document circulation

  • How will cost invoices reach your accounting? Define a new process.
  • Who collects invoices from KSeF and how often?
  • Who is responsible for verifying the correctness of received invoices?
  • How do you submit invoices to the accounting office - or via the CTI Customer Dashboard?

4 Contractor data

  • Complete the Tax Identification Numbers of all contractors - without the Tax Identification Number it is impossible to issue an invoice in KSeF.
  • Verify the correctness of data in the contractor database.
  • Check whether company names are consistent with the register (VIES, Central Statistical Office).

5 Procedures and training

  • Prepare instructions for issuing invoices in the new system for employees.
  • Establish a procedure for dealing with errors and rejections by KSeF.
  • Train the sales department, administration and people issuing invoices.
  • Designate a person responsible for monitoring the KSeF system.

Receipt of cost invoices and document circulation

Error 1. Treating KSeF as a "module" to be installed

KSeF requires changing processes, not just clicking "enable integration". Companies that installed the module without changing document flow found themselves in chaos already in the first week.

Error 2. Failure to receive cost invoices

The company focuses on issuing invoices and forgets about receiving. Effect: cost invoices remain uncollected in KSeF, they do not reach the accounting, and the costs are not recognized. VAT charged, but deduction impossible.

Error 3. Too wide or too narrow scope of permissions

Too wide access - risk of incorrect invoices being issued by unauthorized employees. Too narrow - operational blockade when the authorized person is absent. Permissions require a well-thought-out structure.

Mistake 4. Lack of system integration

Manually "pasting" data from KSeF to an accounting or invoicing program is a recipe for errors and delays. API integration is crucial for smooth operation.

The most common organizational mistakes when implementing KSeF

Issuing sales invoices

The program (e.g. Comarch ERP Optima) sends the invoice to KSeF via API. KSeF assigns a number and sends confirmation. The invoice is automatically entered into the VAT register.

Receipt of cost invoices

The program periodically downloads invoices from KSeF via API. Invoices are automatically sent to the VAT purchase register and to the accounting office via the CTI Customer Desktop.

Zero manual operations

The entire workflow is automated. The accounting office sees invoices in real time and can constantly monitor the completeness of the documentation.

Can you prepare at the last minute?

Theoretically yes. Practically - the later, the greater the operational chaos. The experience of companies that waited until the last moment shows that the first weeks after the obligation entered into force were very difficult - problems with authorizations, missing contractors' NIPs, non-integrated systems and confused employees.

The optimal moment of preparation is several months before the obligation enters into force — to test the integration, train the team and eliminate problems before the cut-off date.

FAQ – most frequently asked questions

Does the PDF of the invoice remain a supporting document after the implementation of KSeF?

No. The only legally binding invoice is the XML file adopted by KSeF. The PDF is only an auxiliary visualization - it does not have the legal value of an invoice.

Can I issue invoices outside KSeF?

No, except for strictly defined exceptions (safe mode when the system is unavailable). After the obligation enters into force, all B2B invoices must go through KSeF.

Do I need to organize the receipt of cost invoices from KSeF?

Yes, collecting invoices from KSeF is an obligation. Cost invoices will no longer be sent via an encrypted channel - they must be downloaded via an integrated program or directly from the ksef.gov.pl portal.

Does KSeF influence the organization of VAT settlements?

Yes. KSeF changes the moment when VAT tax liability arises. The invoice only exists after it has been accepted by KSeF, which affects the date on which the VAT obligation arises.

Summary

KSeF is not a technical change - it is a change in the way the company operates. Document circulation, responsibility and financial control. Companies that prepare in advance will organize their processes and gain full control over documents. Companies that ignore preparation will operate in chaos during the first months of the obligation - with a real risk of VAT errors and loss of tax costs.
