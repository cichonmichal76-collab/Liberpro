KSeF 2026 –
what changes for the entrepreneur and data circulation

The e-invoice system has been operational since 2026. We explain what changes for the entrepreneur, document circulation and data organization in the company.

KSeF VAT March 30, 2026 Liber Pro Accounting

In practice, the National e-Invoice System changes not only the method of issuing documents, but also their receipt, recording and storage. For an entrepreneur, the most important things are: organization of invoice circulation, data consistency with accounting and preparation of internal processes to work on structured documents.

Why was KSeF created and how it is changing data circulation

Poland loses tens of billions of PLNs annually on the so-called VAT gap - the difference between what should flow into the budget from the goods and services tax and what actually does. Tax carousels, fictitious invoices, lowering the tax base - these are phenomena well known to both tax officials and honest entrepreneurs who are put in a worse market position due to unfair practices of competitors.

KSeF is the answer to this problem - but not by increasing the number of controls, but by radically changing the architecture of information flow. Instead of waiting for a VAT return submitted after the fact, tax authorities gain insight into every invoice issued between companies in almost real time. This is a fundamental change in the logic of tax supervision - from reactive to preventive.

Implementation schedule – who and when it covers

The legislator adopted a staged approach, taking into account the different technological readiness of companies of different sizes.

  • From February 1, 2026 — the obligation to issue invoices by KSeF applies to the largest taxpayers whose sales including VAT exceeded PLN 200 million in 2024. The receipt of invoices by KSeF has become mandatory for all VAT taxpayers from this date - regardless of the stage of issuing.
  • From April 1, 2026 — KSeF covers all other active VAT entrepreneurs. The exception is the micro-segment with monthly sales not exceeding PLN 10,000 - for them the obligation to issue e-invoices is postponed until January 1, 2027.
  • From 2027 — administrative penalties apply for errors in the application of the system. The year 2026 is officially a transition period without sanctions - but this does not mean there will be no financial consequences for the budget, as we write about below.

An e-invoice is not a PDF - which changes technically

The biggest misunderstanding around KSeF concerns what an e-invoice is. This is not a PDF document sent by email, even if it looks identical on screen. An e-invoice is a file in XML format with a precisely defined structure, sent directly to the central system of the Ministry of Finance, which assigns it a unique identification number - KSeF ID.

Only a document with this number is considered an invoice within the meaning of tax law. Issuing a PDF invoice and sending it by e-mail will not be sufficient in B2B relations between active VAT payers.

For companies using professional accounting software - such as Comarch ERP Optima - the change is relatively mild, because system manufacturers provide updates supporting integration with KSeF. Companies that keep records in spreadsheets or use outdated systems have a more difficult time.

An entrepreneur's perspective - what changes in everyday work

The implementation of KSeF is not only a technical issue for the company, but above all an organizational one. Internal processes are changing - from the moment an invoice is issued, through its transmission, to archiving.

The costs will include: updating or replacing software, training employees, and in some cases, changing document flow patterns. Companies that issue invoices manually or through simple online programs may need to significantly change their work tools.

On the side of benefits - and it is worth being honest here - there are fewer of them for the entrepreneur himself than for the administration. There will be no risk of losing an invoice or a dispute over whether the document has reached the contractor. The system automatically confirms receipt. But the real benefit in the form of acceleration of VAT refunds, which was promised in previous announcements about KSeF, is not yet guaranteed by the regulations.

How to prepare data organization in the company

This question was asked by many observers when the Ministry of Finance informed the European Commission about the expected revenues from the KSeF of approximately PLN 1.65 billion including VAT already in 2026 - in a year that was officially supposed to be sanction-free.

The mechanism is simpler than it seems. KSeF does not have to impose fines to generate revenues. It is enough that tax authorities receive access to transaction data in close to real time. This allows for the analysis of discrepancies between issued invoices and submitted VAT returns before the deadline for their submission has passed - and not only during an inspection carried out several years later.

In other words: the mere awareness of entrepreneurs that each invoice is immediately visible to the tax office reduces the scale of irregularities. The preventive effect works without the need to impose any penalty. The government estimates VAT revenues for 2026 at PLN 341.5 billion - in this amount, KSeF is to play a significant role both by sealing current settlements and by faster detection of tax refund fraud.

JPK CIT and KSeF – two areas of data quality

Simultaneously with KSeF, from January 1, 2026, companies that are CIT taxpayers submitting JPK-VAT have been subject to the obligation to report JPK_CIT - structured accounting records submitted electronically to the tax office. This is another element of the digitalization of tax supervision, supplementing data from KSeF with information from accounting books.

The combination of both sources - invoices from KSeF and records from JPK_CIT - gives the tax authorities an almost complete picture of the company's activities without the need to carry out a traditional audit.

What should an entrepreneur do now?

If your company is an active VAT payer and has not yet implemented KSeF support, the priority is to check whether the software you use is updated to enable the issuance and receipt of structured invoices. Most major systems on the market already have such integration.

It is also worth remembering that even if your company enters the second stage (from April 1, 2026) or uses the exception for micro-enterprises, from February 1, 2026, you must be ready to receive invoices from large contractors by KSeF. This is a requirement independent of the stage of issuing.

  • Check if your accounting system supports KSeF - if not, contact your supplier or consider changing the tool
  • Train employees responsible for issuing and receiving invoices
  • Adapt your internal document flow processes to the new model
  • If you sell to large companies, check whether your contractor no longer requires KSeF ID on their documents

Summary

KSeF changes the rules of the game on both sides - for entrepreneurs it means the need to adapt processes and tools, and for the state it opens new possibilities for effective tax collection without expanding the control apparatus. The sanction-free year 2026 is not a year without consequences - the system mechanism itself acts as a passive sealant, reducing the space for irregularities even without active actions by officials.

For honest entrepreneurs who settle their accounts reliably, KSeF should not be a source of concern - it is primarily a change in the format of documents and the channel for sending them. The key is appropriate technical and organizational preparation.
