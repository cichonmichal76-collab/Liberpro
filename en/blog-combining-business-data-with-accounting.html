Bringing business data and accounting together

How to organise data and use it to understand your business better.

Data analytics8 August 2026Liber Pro Accounting

Important information is often spread across several systems. Sales data may sit in a sales platform, CRM or online shop. Project information may be held elsewhere, marketing spend on advertising platforms, and financial and settlement data in the accounting system.

Each source shows only part of the picture. Sales alone do not explain margin, costs, liabilities or cash flow.

Why combine data sources?

Consolidating data in one tool makes it possible to compare operational information with accounting data. Instead of reviewing sales reports, CRM exports and cost spreadsheets separately, a business can build a clearer view of its key indicators.

Depending on the available data, this may include sales and costs by month, margin by product, service or project, marketing expenditure against sales, payroll, tax and social-insurance costs, liabilities and expected payments.

From scattered reports to useful information

The aim is not to create another complicated report. The aim is to make information easier to use, so the business owner can understand the situation and identify areas that need attention sooner.

Combining data can help assess whether sales growth translates into results, how costs are changing, or whether a planned decision requires additional financial preparation.

How can we help?

At Liber Pro, we want to combine accounting services with data analytics. We can help organise data from different systems, consolidate it in one tool and compare it with accounting data.

If you use several systems and want to understand your business more clearly, we can help create a more complete picture of sales, costs, margin, liabilities and cash flow.
