Start-up relief and preferential ZUS – how the next stages work

Check the conditions for using the start-up relief, preferential ZUS and Mały ZUS Plus contributions, as well as reporting obligations when setting up a company.

ZUS May 10, 2018 Liber Pro Accounting

When starting a business, it is important not only to choose the form of taxation, but also to correctly report to ZUS and determine which reliefs can be used in a given period. From the accounting office's perspective, the key factors are: correct reporting codes, meeting deadlines and proper transition between subsequent stages of relief.

Three stages of lower ZUS contributions for new entrepreneurs

A new entrepreneur can go through three subsequent stages of reduced contributions before starting to pay the full ZUS:

  • Stage 1. Start-up relief: 6 months without social security contributions (you only pay health insurance contributions)
  • Stage 2. Preferential ZUS contributions: 24 months of contributions calculated from 30% of the minimum wage
  • Stage 3. Small ZUS Plus: contributions calculated on income (for companies with revenue up to PLN 120,000)

Stage 1. Start-up relief (6 months)

The start-up relief exempts a new entrepreneur from social security contributions (pension, disability, sickness, accident) for the first 6 months of running a business. You only pay health insurance premium.

In 2026, the minimum health insurance contribution for entrepreneurs settling on a straight-line basis or on general terms is approximately PLN 381 per month (9% of the base = PLN 4,242). For comparison, full ZUS amounts to over PLN 1,800 per month. Savings on starting tax relief: approx. PLN 1,400-1,500 per month for 6 months, i.e. over PLN 8,500 in total.

Who can benefit from the start-up relief?

  • An individual setting up a JDG for the first time lub who has not conducted business in the last 60 months
  • Who will not provide services to the former employer (with whom she had an employment contract in the last 12 months) in the scope of the same activities as previously on a full-time basis

Note – disadvantages of the start-up tax relief

  • No pension insurance contributions - you do not save for your future retirement
  • No sickness insurance - you will not receive sickness or maternity benefits if you get sick or give birth to a child during this period

Therefore, it is worth considering whether to use the relief - if you are planning a pregnancy or have health problems, it is better to pay your sickness insurance premiums right away.

Stage 2. Preferential ZUS contributions (24 months)

After 6 months of starting relief (or immediately if you do not use it), you can: 24 months pay social contributions from a reduced base. 30% of the minimum salary instead of 60% of the forecast salary (standard basis).

In 2026, the minimum wage is PLN 4,666 gross. 30% of this is the base PLN 1,399.80. On this basis, social contributions amount to approx. PLN 580–620 per month (instead of approximately PLN 1,400 with full ZUS). You pay your health insurance premium independently.

Stage 3. Small ZUS Plus – additional rules

After 24 months of preferential contributions, you can benefit from Mały ZUS Plus - if your income from the previous year did not exceed PLN 120,000. In Mały ZUS Plus, social contributions are calculated based on income (not income), which results in very low contributions if the income is low.

You can use Mały ZUS Plus for: a maximum of 36 months within 60 consecutive months running a business.

How to properly report to ZUS?

When registering a business in CEIDG, an application to ZUS can be submitted in the same application - however, you must select the appropriate insurance title code:

  • Start-up discount: code 0570 (health insurance only). must be reported within 7 days from the date of commencement of business
  • Preferential contributions: code 0590. deregister from 0570 and register on 0590 after 6 months
  • Full ZUS or Small ZUS Plus: code 0510 or appropriate for MZP

An incorrect code or late reporting may result in the need to pay additional premiums with interest. Therefore, it is worth entrusting the registration and first reports to ZUS to an accounting office.

Summary

The ZUS relief system for new entrepreneurs provides real savings of PLN 20,000-30,000 for the first 2.5 years. It is crucial that you report correctly and on time to the correct insurance code.
