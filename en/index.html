What we do

Comprehensive service finansowa

From the first day of operation to a mature business - one supervisor, full service, no surprises.

Full accounting

KH, KPiR, VAT, CIT, PIT, JPK, financial statements

We provide full accounting services tailored to your business. We know the regulations, we take care of the deadlines and eliminate the risk of errors.

  • Maintaining accounting books and KPiR in accordance with the Accounting Act
  • VAT, CIT, PIT declarations and sending JPK files to the tax office
  • Records of fixed assets, depreciation and inventory
  • Financial reports for the management board, Central Statistical Office, National Bank of Poland and banks
  • Representation before tax offices and ZUS
Find out more →

HR and Payroll

Personal files, payrolls, ZUS, PFRON, contracts

We take over all HR and payroll responsibilities, so you can focus on running your business. We constantly monitor changes in labor law.

  • Maintaining personal files in accordance with the Labor Code
  • Calculating salaries, payrolls and calculating ZUS contributions
  • ZUS declarations: DRA, RCA, RZA and employee declarations
  • Managing PPK and preparing PIT-11 for employees
Find out more →

Business registration

JDG, CEIDG, NIP, REGON, ZUS, choice of form of taxation

We will guide you through the entire company registration process - from choosing the form of taxation to launching full accounting services from the first day of operation.

  • Preparation and submission of the CEIDG application - obtaining the NIP and REGON
  • Assistance in choosing the form of taxation and PKD codes
  • Applications to ZUS and registration as a VAT payer
  • Configuration of the accounting system from day one
Find out more →

Company registration

KRS, company deed, NIP, REGON, ZUS - full registration

Comprehensive support in establishing companies - from preparation of the contract to registration in the National Court Register and implementation of full accounting services.

  • Preparation of registration documents and company agreement
  • Registration in the National Court Register – S24 system or notary
  • Obtaining the NIP, REGON and registration with ZUS as a payer
  • Full accounting services for the company from day one
